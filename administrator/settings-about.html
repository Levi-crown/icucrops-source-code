About Us

ICuCrops - An IoT Web-Based System for Crops Cultivation and Monitoring

ICuCrops

Helps farmers monitor and manage crop cultivation through real-time readings for soil moisture, soil pH, soil nutrients, temperature, and humidity.

Developed for Sarate Agricultural Farm in San Pablo, Laguna, the system supports faster checking, organized farm data, better resource use, and healthier crop growth.

Our Mission

To provide farmers with a reliable and accessible smart farming system that supports efficient crop monitoring and cultivation management.

Our Vision

To promote modern, technology-based farming practices that improve productivity, sustainability, and crop health.
